```
S-Tier Eats ranks the restaurants of The Woodlands and north Houston the way you actually think about them — not with five stars that smear every place into a forgettable 3.9-to-4.4 blob, but on a clean S/A/B/C/F tier list.

Place a spot in S ("drop everything, go now") or F ("never again"), and instantly see where the community lands too.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Browse 4,700+ restaurants across The Woodlands, Spring, Conroe, Tomball, Klein, Cypress, Kingwood and more
• Explore them on a fast, clustered map
• Build your own S/A/B/C/F tier list that syncs across your devices
• See the community consensus — how everyone (or just the Foodie Pros) ranks each place
• Filter and search by name, cuisine, or area
• Check crowd-sourced dietary tags: gluten-free, vegetarian, vegan, allergy-aware, halal, kid-friendly, kosher
• Mark the places you've been

WHY IT'S DIFFERENT
No endless feed. No follower counts. No influencer noise. No personalized tracking. Just a sharp, honest ranking of where to eat, built by someone who actually lives here and eats here.

Sign in with Google to rank restaurants and have your tiers follow you across devices — browsing and the community boards are open to everyone, no account needed.

Free to use. Made in The Woodlands.
```
