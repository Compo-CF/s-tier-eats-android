# Play Console — Content rating (IARC) questionnaire answers

Complete under Policy → App content → **Content ratings**. The IARC
questionnaire generates ESRB / PEGI / USK / etc. ratings from your answers.
Answer honestly for the CURRENT Android release (no ads, no photos, no
user-visible free text, no messaging).

## Category
- Choose **Reference, News, or Educational** *or* **Utility, Productivity,
  Communication, or Other** — S-Tier Eats is a restaurant reference/utility
  app. (Do **not** pick "Social" — there is no messaging or user-visible UGC.)
